# Games club — direction contract

THESIS: A browser card game that feels like sitting down at a well-made games table. The cards are the content; getting into a game is the primary task.
OWN-WORLD: Warm paper, charcoal type, vermilion actions, forest playing surface. Rounded physical cards with clear indices and bold symbols. Sans typography, fine rules, compact utility controls.
STORY: Choose bots or friends, set seats, deal, match the active color or value, call UNO, and play again.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim wordmark and utility bar. Large left-aligned invitation to play; a full-height right-hand green card table with a generous fan of actual game cards. Two explicit play actions, then a compact friends strip. On mobile the table becomes a shallow banner and actions stay above the fold.
FORM: Grounded direction 7, games-club membership and playing table, seed c740dd9e. Alternatives considered: match scorepad, card packaging, sports broadcast, family game cabinet, travel card deck, tournament bracket. The specimen challenger donated typographic clarity; dance notation donated a stable spatial axis; catalog donated consistent player identities. Other challengers declined for weaker game clarity. User delegated final design decisions; this session uses code-first without changing future workflow defaults.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
