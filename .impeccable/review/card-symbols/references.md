No external card scans ship in the app. The source-authored SVGs reproduce the functional printed motifs, not pixel-perfect commercial artwork. Mystery cards, not a physical roulette wheel, identify Color Roulette; opposite diagonal arrows identify Reverse; circular arrow identifies Skip Everyone; two overlapping Skip marks identify Skip Two; target+2 is centered within the target; fan plus exchange arrow identifies Forced Swap. Draw amounts remain in corner indices while centers show counted card stacks. All Wild action ovals use the existing four deck colors.
