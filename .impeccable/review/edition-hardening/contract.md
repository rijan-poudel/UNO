# Three editions — completion and hardening

THESIS: The approved home remains a clear start screen. Select one of three favorites, then play, create, or join. Settings opens one task at a time.
OWN-WORLD: Preserve Outfit, Barlow Condensed, cream lettering, native four-color card faces, amber actions, ready-made portraits, and the user's approved blue home. No new identity or imagery. No Mercy inherits charcoal/crimson; All Wild inherits plum. Boards follow their edition.
STORY: Home favorites → game. Avatar → Game settings → Game rules → choose a profile, pin it, or open a custom-copy editor. Original editions are fixed. Favorites and active identity are independent. Only a named custom copy exposes house-rule controls.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Existing card fan and Play/Create/Join remain. Three compact favorites use short edition labels. Game rules is a flat readable list with an active checkmark and labeled star/copy controls. Do not render all rule toggles in the list. A custom editor owns its native dialog with a scrolling body and fixed Save and use / Cancel actions; the parent Done action is inaccessible while editing.
FORM: Constrained code-led refinement and functional repair of the supplied build, not a replacement visual world. Native dialogs retain focus, keyboard and responsive behavior; no new dependencies. Original card art/textures/provenance remain untouched.
FINISH: Fresh finish review, required checks, desktop/phone/short-landscape captures, then merge factual design documentation. No approved new comp applies; the incumbent user-approved world is the reference.

## Quality bar

Operate mode for settings; Experience for play. Broken tasks outrank ornament. No speculative visual overhaul. Single-task disclosure, honest labels, readable targets, no clipping, and fixed decisions at short heights. Keep ready-made avatars, audio, friendship and code-only joining. No Appearance section or Saved on this device slogan. Historical custom avatars remain compatibility data only.

## Functional scope

Fix the actual homepage React snapshot crash; isolate custom drafts; preserve one selected identity even for identical copies; canonicalize original IDs over multiplayer. Correct No Mercy recipient-owned Roulette, target selection, two-survivor Reverse +4, elimination/rotation/recycling, scoring and recovery. Enforce the drawn-card restriction in All Wild too. New edition help and labels must reflect actual actions. Existing turn deadlines and per-round wins are retained; full official tournament scoring/bluff challenges are not claimed.
