User-pinned reference: Duolingo's playfulness, responsive microinteractions and emotional feedback. Extend the shipped Outfit / Barlow native-card world with original card-face expressions; do not copy Duo, introduce a new font or replace table materials. Code-led local extension, no approved comp.

Player expressions reflect real snapshots: thinking on their turn, surprise after a multi-card penalty, delight on a successful play/UNO, celebration for the winner. Existing avatar grammar carries this, including narrow phones. No fake XP, pressure streak, idle animation loop or extra navigation. The launch fan gets the same original characters. Primary choices and color swatches have a visible pressed surface. Bounded motion: a single short expression change, pointer-fine hover responses only, no spatial expression animation with reduced motion. All actual counts, action names, native dialog focus, first-tap play and accessibility stay authoritative. Verify desktop, 390px and 320px six-seat/large-hand states and sideways phone in one batch.
