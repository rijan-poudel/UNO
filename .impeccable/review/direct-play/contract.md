## Signature interaction
Tap a legal card to play it immediately; nonmatching cards can be lifted to inspect. Drag vertically to lift an actual card from the fan. A visible table landing area appears while dragging; release above the bounded upward threshold plays, return/cancel restores the card. Horizontal scrolling does not play. Keyboard activation plays legal cards; arrow navigation, D/P/U remain. No action waits for animation or audio. UNO and pass are contextual; Draw lives on the deck, with a dock fallback only when a penalty needs explanation.

## Motion and sound grammar
Pickup is immediate paper movement, with a short discrete pickup sound (not a note on every hover). Play travels from the actual release position to the pile with a fast arc and contact. The discard retains the previous two faces. Draw and deal originate from the deck; no duplicate endpoint while a card is flying. The table exposes readiness during drag rather than an unrelated floating badge. Special actions carry brief graphic signals. Reduced motion removes flights, tilt and moving emphasis while retaining state and direct play. Sound unlock happens in the same gesture as pickup; sound never gates or queues turns. Muted, zero-volume, hidden, expired and offline behavior must pass assertions. Do not claim physical speaker quality without listening.
