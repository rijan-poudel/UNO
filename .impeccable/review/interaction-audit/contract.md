# Motion and interaction repair contract

Preserve Outfit/Barlow lettering, native vivid deck faces, real baize/leather and direct first-activation play. The user explicitly delegates the full audit and fixes; no approval gate is needed.

Focal moment: the physical card travels from its actual release pose to the discard at its actual size and angle. The table itself stays still. Continuity: surviving cards reflow in their fan; new cards arrive from the real deck (redistributed shuffle cards from the played pile), without duplicate endpoints or a backlog after interruption. Feedback: immediate action sound, visible draw/last-card counts, meaningful reduced-motion action fades, and keyboard focus retained in the hand. Budget: transform/opacity only, 190–262ms flight, at most six staggered local arrivals, no turn or voice-duration gating. Pickup belongs to pickup/inspection, not scrolling.

Phone: side-by-side table and hand in landscape; compact portrait for short phones. Primary actions use dark ink on existing lime inside the fixed arena. All shell themes and the existing game/rule/history boundaries remain.

Verification: one batched phone/desktop inspection plus one correction/confirmation pass, detector once, mandatory lint/types/game/audio/build/socket checks, documented limitations, commit/push and live smoke. No new raster asset or replacement comp required.
